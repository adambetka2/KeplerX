# Restored Rust catalogue and follow-up

On October 26, 2026, the PI withdrew manual review as a membership filter for
the first Rust catalogue. Its scientific membership is all 147 frozen groups
(156 proposals, 116 sources). The [current selection](../results/boi-validation/boi-current-selection-20261026.json)
restores 33 initial NO choices and BOI-2103289985012370304-1, the later delivery
removal. Historical review, delivery and validation records remain unchanged.

## Review of the 33 initial exclusions

The [parameter table](../results/boi-validation/excluded-rust-review-20261026/parameters.csv)
and [evidence record](../results/boi-validation/excluded-rust-review-20261026/parameters.json)
retain the exact clocks, original views, durations and event counts. No manual
reason or note was recorded for these 33 decisions.

Saved original displays cover 27 groups. Six further displays use read-only
native SAP or PSF columns, quality-zero finite cadences, positive quarter-median
normalization and the original clocks. These displays have no fitted detrending
or calibrated significance. Their preparation differs from discovery preparation.

Three clocks are compatible with same-source subharmonics: BOI-2077554781499350144-2
(approximately one-third of the retained period), BOI-2077967368932890752-3
(one-seventh), and BOI-2126354436132009088-2 (one-third). Compatibility is not
proof that no independent signal exists; test event independence before a new
probability campaign.

Twenty-seven displays show broad or asymmetric modulation. Three merit targeted
native-model and ownership review: BOI-2079376637907047040-1,
BOI-2126446588949629952-1 and BOI-2131801043071611776-1. These are follow-up
priorities, not physical false-positive classifications or catalogue exclusions.
V-shaped transits, short periods and large supplied durations alone do not deny
a planet. No stellar-radius or planet-size inference is fabricated from a plot.

The [catalogue screen](../results/boi-validation/excluded-rust-review-20261026/catalogue-screen.json)
uses the existing October 25 DR25 TCE and KOI responses. Nearby period/epoch
associations require source-ownership checks, including KIC5199436 near the
KIC5199422 signal and KIC9226439 near KIC9226435. KIC8758214 has a compatible
catalogue false-positive signal about 72 arcsec away. Associations and chance
match expectations are recorded; no contamination mechanism is established.
This screen does not close the exact-signal literature gate or establish novelty.

## Readiness queue

All 34 restored groups enter the [readiness queue](../results/boi-validation/restored-rust-readiness-queue-20261026.json).
It is separate from the active native-fit/probability coordinator. No restored
group has yet passed all preparation gates for automatic probability allocation.

An exact original SAP proposal snapshot for KIC7989202 has search MES 48.67,
MES 26.70 and local MES 14.06. These are different original statistics; they do
not replace an accepted native fit. Its broad modulation needs scientific review.
Original detection significance remains unavailable for the other 32 initial
exclusions in the available lean evidence.

The [new SAP diagnostic](../results/boi-validation/excluded-rust-review-20261026/restored-sap-diagnostics.json)
covers all 34 restored groups. It measures original-clock local depths and their
variation between quarters, with opposite-phase and shoulder comparisons. Its
quarter-consistency S/N is uncalibrated and is not a detector threshold, native
posterior significance or rejection rule. No restored signal is parked merely
because this diagnostic is low.

A new exact-ID Gaia DR3 query recovered all 34 targets: 32 have RUWE at most
1.4, two exceed it, and 10 have reported paired FLAME mass and radius. Reported
intervals remain attached to the readiness records. These are host-property
checks, not complete scene or validation closure. The separate union-of-cones
query timed out at the public service with HTTP 408; its request and failed
response are retained. The target-only query does not substitute for that scene.

Before adding a case to the existing durable coordinator, authenticate the full
scene and actual source apertures; complete exact-signal, alias and event checks;
then prepare and fit native photons under the adopted V3 protocol. Existing
scientific gates and integration budgets remain unchanged. The external evidence
disc had only 179 MiB free, below the project's 2 GiB reserve, when this queue was
created. New large acquisitions await suitable evidence storage.

The PI subsequently authorized the internal disc. The
[storage authorization](../results/boi-validation/internal-evidence-storage-20261026.json)
clears this storage blocker for new preparations under
`other/boi-v3-20261025/`. Original external data remain read-only; scientific
readiness requirements are unchanged.
