# Light-curve atlas

Each folder contains only `light-curve.png` and `properties.json`: photometric features, sources and lookalikes. These are examples from astronomical papers.

- [Earth-sized: Kepler-20 e](examples/planet-earth-sized-e/light-curve.png) - extremely shallow recurring dips; [Earth-sized: Kepler-20 f](examples/planet-earth-sized-f/light-curve.png) - shallow, less frequent transits.
- [Super-Earth: Kepler-20 b](examples/planet-super-earth/light-curve.png) - deeper short-period transits; [six-planet system: Kepler-11](examples/multiple-planets/light-curve.png) - six periods, separate folds and per-planet statistics.

- [Hot-Jupiter transit](examples/planet-hot-jupiter/light-curve.png) - repeated narrow dips; [shallow planet transit](examples/planet-shallow/light-curve.png) - weak dips revealed by folding.
- [White-dwarf eclipses: KOI-74](examples/white-dwarf-eclipsing/light-curve.png) - transit plus deeper occultation; [white-dwarf self-lensing: KOI-3278](examples/white-dwarf-self-lensing/light-curve.png) - recurring brightening and separate occultation.
- [Black-hole microlensing: OGLE-2011-BLG-0462](examples/black-hole-microlensing/light-curve.png) - long magnification event; published BH identification also uses astrometry.
- [White-dwarf planet-candidate transit](examples/planet-white-dwarf/light-curve.png) - deep, minute-long dips; [circumbinary transit](examples/circumbinary-transit/light-curve.png) - eclipses plus transits with variable timing/duration.
- [Disintegrating-planet candidate](examples/disintegrating-planet/light-curve.png) - variable-depth asymmetric dips; [exocomet-like dip](examples/exocomet/light-curve.png) - isolated shallow asymmetric fading.
- [White-dwarf debris](examples/white-dwarf-debris/light-curve.png) - changing deep dips, hours apart; [ring-system candidate](examples/ring-occultation/light-curve.png) - prolonged structured occultation.
- [Detached binary](examples/detached-eclipsing/light-curve.png) - separated eclipses, flat baseline; [β Lyrae-like binary](examples/beta-lyrae/light-curve.png) - unequal eclipses, continuous variation.
- [Contact binary](examples/contact-binary/light-curve.png) - continuous double wave; [ellipsoidal variable](examples/ellipsoidal/light-curve.png) - smooth double wave without sharp eclipses.
- [Heartbeat binary](examples/heartbeat/light-curve.png) - brief asymmetric orbital feature; [reflection binary](examples/reflection-binary/light-curve.png) - broad heating wave plus eclipses.
- [RS CVn-like binary](examples/rs-cvn/light-curve.png) - eclipses plus evolving modulation; [double-periodic variable](examples/double-periodic/light-curve.png) - orbital variability plus a slower cycle.
- [δ Scuti](examples/delta-scuti/light-curve.png) - rapid coherent pulsation; [γ Doradus](examples/gamma-doradus/light-curve.png) - slower multiperiodic beating.
- [Hybrid pulsator](examples/hybrid-pulsator/light-curve.png) - low/high-frequency modes together; [SPB](examples/spb/light-curve.png) - slow beating, resembles γ Dor.
- [roAp](examples/roap/light-curve.png) - minute-scale oscillations; [hot-subdwarf pulsator](examples/hot-subdwarf/light-curve.png) - rapid, multiple frequencies.
- [Pulsating white dwarf](examples/white-dwarf-pulsator/light-curve.png) - minute-scale multiperiodicity; [BLAP](examples/blap/light-curve.png) - rapid, large-amplitude pulsation.
- [RRab](examples/rrab/light-curve.png) - steep rise, slow decline; [RRc](examples/rrc/light-curve.png) - smoother sub-day wave; [RRd](examples/rrd/light-curve.png) - two pulsation modes.
- [Classical Cepheid](examples/cepheid-fundamental/light-curve.png) - coherent asymmetric pulsation; [anomalous Cepheid](examples/anomalous-cepheid/light-curve.png) - similar short-period waveform.
- [BL Herculis](examples/bl-her/light-curve.png) - short-period type-II Cepheid; [W Virginis](examples/w-vir/light-curve.png) - longer, structured pulsation.
- [RV Tauri](examples/rv-tauri/light-curve.png) - alternating minima; [Mira](examples/mira/light-curve.png) - large, long-period oscillations.
- [Semiregular variable](examples/semiregular/light-curve.png) - drifting cycles; [OSARG](examples/osarg/light-curve.png) - small overlapping long-period variations.
- [Long secondary period](examples/long-secondary-period/light-curve.png) - slow modulation plus faster variability; [solar-like oscillations](examples/solar-like-giant/light-curve.png) - weak stochastic multimode power.
- [Starspot rotation](examples/starspot-rotation/light-curve.png) - broad evolving modulation; [stellar flare](examples/flare/light-curve.png) - rapid brightening, slower decay.
- [Young-star dipper](examples/yso-dipper/light-curve.png) - changing dust dips; [young-star burster](examples/yso-burster/light-curve.png) - irregular positive bursts; [young-star spots](examples/yso-spots/light-curve.png) - broad repetitive modulation.
- [R Coronae Borealis](examples/r-coronae-borealis/light-curve.png) - deep fading, prolonged recovery; [Boyajian’s / Tabby’s Star](examples/boyajian/light-curve.png) - KIC 8462852; irregular complex dips.
- [Dwarf nova](examples/dwarf-nova/light-curve.png) - recurring accretion outbursts; [FU Orionis-type](examples/fu-orionis/light-curve.png) - sustained large brightening.
- [Brown-dwarf weather](examples/brown-dwarf-weather/light-curve.png) - rapidly evolving rotational modulation; [optical pulsar](examples/optical-pulsar/light-curve.png) - millisecond double-pulse profile.
- [Planetary microlensing](examples/planetary-microlensing/light-curve.png) - broad magnification plus short anomaly; [Type Ia supernova](examples/supernova-ia/light-curve.png) - one rise, band-dependent decline; [AGN](examples/agn/light-curve.png) - correlated aperiodic variations.

Separate synthetic references: [theoretical objects and modeled compact-object signals](theoretical/).
