# KeplerX

KeplerX is a modern toolset for discovering exoplanets.

It couldn't have been built without the existence of decades of institutional knowledge of astronomy, papers and toolings prepared by the amazing authors:

- [TRASHDUMP and RECYCLEBin](https://github.com/robertfwilson/trashdump) — Robert F. Wilson
- [INCInERATOr](https://github.com/juliemoqs/incinerator) — Julie Moquin, Robert F. Wilson, Jorge Martínez-Palomera and Christina Hedges
- [TRICERATOPS](https://github.com/stevengiacalone/triceratops) — Steven Giacalone
- [batman](https://github.com/lkreidberg/batman) — Laura Kreidberg
- [dynesty](https://github.com/joshspeagle/dynesty) — Joshua S. Speagle and Sergey E. Koposov
- [juliet](https://github.com/nespinoza/juliet) — Néstor Espinoza
- [PyTransit](https://github.com/hpparvi/PyTransit) — Hannu Parviainen
- [wotan](https://github.com/hippke/wotan) — Michael Hippke
- [lkprf](https://github.com/lightkurve/lkprf) — Christina Hedges and the TESS Science Support Center
- [lamatrix](https://github.com/christinahedges/lamatrix) — Christina Hedges
- [Lightkurve](https://github.com/lightkurve/lightkurve) — Geert Barentsen and the Lightkurve Collaboration
- [Kepler Bonus (KBonus) light curves](https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/kbonus-bkg) — Jorge Martínez-Palomera, Christina Hedges and Jessie Dotson
- [Mean dwarf stellar color and effective temperature sequence](https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~emamajek/EEM_dwarf_UBVIJHK_colors_Teff.txt) — Eric Mamajek (Pecaut & Mamajek 2013)
- [Astropy](https://www.astropy.org) and [astroquery](https://github.com/astropy/astroquery) — the Astropy and astroquery developers
